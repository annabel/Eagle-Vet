Senior Pet Wellness Month

November is Senior Pet Wellness Month, and wellness care for an older pet can go beyond their yearly exam and bloodwork.

Radiographs can be a helpful preventative tool for senior pets because they can give us a look at areas we can’t evaluate during a physical exam alone.

Depending on what we are screening for, X-rays can help us look for things like arthritis, dysplasia, and degenerative joint disease in the joints. Abdominal radiographs can help identify changes such as enlarged organs or masses, while chest X-rays can help screen for things like an enlarged heart, enlarged lymph nodes, or lung nodules. …
